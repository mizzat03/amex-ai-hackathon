# Business value and product limits

The investigator shortens the path from a technical payment-health change to an auditable working
hypothesis. It keeps three frequently conflated questions separate: whether technical failures
increased, which traffic is affected, and which observed change best fits the evidence.

The judge-facing workflow makes each conclusion inspectable: current-versus-baseline metrics,
affected-scope complements, dominant error signature, observed operational changes, qualitative
RCA tier, contradictions, limitations and versioned citations. This supports faster, more
consistent engineering triage while preserving accountable human decisions.

The Copilot adds bounded synthesis and next-check guidance but is not required for incident truth.
Its disabled-provider fallback demonstrates operational resilience and avoids making model
availability a condition for viewing evidence or recording review.

This hackathon MVP does not claim production accuracy, revenue impact, universal thresholds,
autonomous remediation, real-card-data readiness, or measured fleet-scale latency/cost. The local
observations are reproducibility evidence only. A production value study should measure time to
correct scope, time to defensible hypothesis, citation-use rate, false-escalation rate and reviewer
agreement against an approved operational baseline.
